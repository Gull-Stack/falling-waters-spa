Who we are

Falling Waters Day Spa & Salon opened in 1998 at 1101 E Draper Parkway, inside Treehouse Athletic Club in Draper, Utah. For more than 27 years we have been the place Draper unwinds: one roof for massage therapy, HydraFacial and custom facials, hair, nails, brows and lashes, waxing, body treatments and couples experiences.

You do not need to be a Treehouse member to visit. Falling Waters is open to the public, and Treehouse Athletic Club members save 10% on every spa and salon service, every visit. Just mention your membership when you book.

Licensed professionals

Every service is performed by a licensed Utah professional: massage therapists, estheticians, nail technicians and hair stylists. Massage happens in private treatment rooms, and our couples suite lets two guests be treated side by side.

What guests say

Guests rate Falling Waters 4.4 out of 5 across 136+ Google reviews. The names that come up most are our long-tenured stylists and therapists, and the calm of a spa tucked inside a club rather than a strip mall.

About Falling Waters FAQ

Do I have to be a Treehouse Athletic Club member to book?

No. Falling Waters is open to the public. Treehouse Athletic Club members receive 10% off all services.

How long has Falling Waters been in Draper?

Since 1998. We have operated inside Treehouse Athletic Club at 1101 E Draper Parkway for more than 27 years.

Are your therapists and stylists licensed?

Yes. Every service is performed by a licensed Utah massage therapist, esthetician, nail technician or hair stylist.

Is parking available?

Yes. Free parking at Treehouse Athletic Club. We are located inside the club.

What is your cancellation policy?

We require 24-hour notice for cancellations. Late cancellations may incur a fee.
