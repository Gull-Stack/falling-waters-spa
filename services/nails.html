Nail Service FAQ

Answers to the questions we hear most

What nail services does Falling Waters offer in Draper?

We offer classic and spa manicures, luxury and spa pedicures, gel manicures, acrylic nail extensions (full sets and fills), dip powder nails, and natural nail strengthening treatments. All services are performed by licensed nail technicians in a clean, relaxing spa environment.

How long do gel nails last compared to regular polish?

Gel nails typically last 2-3 weeks without chipping, compared to regular polish which usually lasts 5-7 days. We use premium gel brands cured under LED for a durable, high-shine finish.

What's the difference between acrylics and dip powder?

Acrylics use liquid monomer and powder to create extensions with maximum length and durability. Dip powder involves dipping nails into pigmented powder — lighter than acrylics, no UV lamp, and generally gentler on natural nails. Both last 2-4 weeks.

Are your nail products safe and non-toxic?

Absolutely. We use salon-professional products free of formaldehyde, toluene, and DBP. All metal tools are hospital-grade sterilized, and we use fresh disposable files, buffers, and liners for every client.

Can I combine nails with other spa services?

Yes! Our mani-pedi combos are very popular, and you can add a massage, facial, or any other service. We also offer spa packages that include nails as part of a full pampering experience.

Do Treehouse Athletic Club members get a discount?

TAC members receive 10% off all nail services (and every other service we offer). Just mention your membership when booking or checking in.
