Brow and lash services in Draper

Brows and lashes frame everything else. At Falling Waters Day Spa in Draper, licensed Utah estheticians shape, tint, lift and extend so you wake up ready, with less time in front of the mirror each morning. Services start at $23, and Treehouse Athletic Club members save 10%.

What we offer

  • Lash extensions: individual synthetic lashes bonded to your natural lashes for length and fullness that lasts weeks, with fills as they grow out.
  • Lash lift: a semi-permanent curl of your own lashes, often paired with a tint. No extensions, no mascara needed, results for 6 to 8 weeks.
  • Brow shaping: waxing or tweezing to a shape that suits your face. Eyebrow waxing is $20 on our waxing menu.
  • Brow and lash tinting: semi-permanent color that defines light or sparse brows and lashes for several weeks.

Before your appointment

Arrive with clean, makeup-free eyes for lash services. Skip waterproof mascara for 24 hours before a lift or extensions. Tell your esthetician about any eye sensitivity or recent eye procedures, and she will patch test a tint when in doubt.
