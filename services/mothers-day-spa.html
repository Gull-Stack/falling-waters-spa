Mother's Day Spa FAQ

Common questions about our Mother's Day packages

When should I book Mom's Mother's Day spa appointment?

We recommend booking 2-3 weeks in advance for Mother's Day weekend. For the most flexibility, consider celebrating the week before or after Mother's Day when we have more availability.

Can I customize the packages for Mom's specific needs?

Absolutely! Every package can be tailored to Mom's preferences, whether she needs deeper massage pressure, has skin sensitivities, or wants to focus on specific areas of tension.

What's included with the champagne service?

Our champagne service includes premium champagne or sparkling cider (non-alcoholic option), gourmet chocolates, and comfortable seating in our relaxation lounge.

Can multiple family members book packages together?

Yes! We love hosting mother-daughter spa days, sisters' celebrations, and multi-generational experiences. Call us to coordinate multiple appointments.

What if Mom can't make it on Mother's Day?

Our packages are available year-round! Many families choose to celebrate before or after the official holiday for a more relaxed, personalized experience.

Do you offer packages for new mothers or mothers with young children?

We understand the unique needs of new moms and can recommend treatments that provide the rest and care they need. We also offer flexible scheduling to work around nursing schedules.
