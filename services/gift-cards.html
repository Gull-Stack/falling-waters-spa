Gift Card Questions

How do I purchase a gift card?

Call us at (801) 501-9000 or visit us in person at 1101 E Draper Parkway, Draper, UT 84020. Gift cards are available in any denomination.

What can gift cards be used for?

Our gift cards can be used for any service — massage therapy, facials, HydraFacial, hair care, nail services, waxing, lash extensions, and spa packages.

Do gift cards expire?

No! Our gift cards never expire. Recipients can use them whenever they're ready to enjoy their spa experience.

Can I buy a gift card for a specific service?

Yes! You can purchase a gift card for a specific service (like a 50-minute Swedish massage or a HydraFacial) or choose a dollar amount and let the recipient pick their own experience.

What are the most popular gift card amounts?

Our most popular: $105 (50-minute massage), $150 (custom facial), $210 (Birthday Glow Experience), $249 (Ultimate Spa Day), and $349 (Bloom & Bliss Couples).
