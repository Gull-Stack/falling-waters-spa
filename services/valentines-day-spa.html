Valentine's Day Spa FAQ

Common questions about our romantic spa packages

How far in advance should we book our Valentine's Day spa package?

Valentine's Day is our most popular romantic holiday. We recommend booking 3-4 weeks in advance for February 14th, or consider celebrating the week before or after for better availability and a more intimate experience.

Can we customize our Valentine's package for special occasions?

Absolutely! Whether you're planning a proposal, celebrating an anniversary, or have specific romantic preferences, we can customize any package to make your celebration perfectly unique.

What's included with the couples massage suite?

All Valentine's packages include a completely private couples massage room with side-by-side tables, romantic lighting, soft music, and comfortable seating for relaxation before and after treatments.

Are your Valentine's packages suitable for first-time spa visitors?

Yes! Our therapists are experienced in making first-time couples feel comfortable. We'll explain everything beforehand and ensure both partners feel relaxed throughout the experience.

Can we extend our spa day with dinner or other activities?

We're happy to help coordinate with local restaurants for post-spa dining. Many couples combine their spa experience with dinner reservations to create a full romantic day.

Do you accommodate same-day Valentine's bookings?

While we encourage advance booking, we do our best to accommodate same-day requests based on availability. Call us at (801) 501-9000 to check current openings.
