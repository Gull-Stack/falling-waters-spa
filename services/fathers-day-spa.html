Father's Day Spa FAQ

Common questions about our Father's Day specials

Can the two-massage packages be used on separate visits?

Yes! Packages 1 and 2 each include two massage sessions. They can be used together as a couples experience or on two separate visits — whatever works best for Dad.

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 1-2 weeks in advance to secure your preferred time. Father's Day week fills up quickly.

What's the difference between Swedish and deep tissue massage?

Swedish massage uses long, flowing strokes for overall relaxation and stress relief. Deep tissue massage uses firmer pressure to target chronic muscle tension and knots — ideal for active dads or those with desk-job tightness.

Can I purchase these as a gift?

Absolutely. Call us at (801) 501-9000 to purchase any package as a gift. We can arrange scheduling or let Dad choose his own appointment time.
