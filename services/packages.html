Spa Packages FAQ

How do I book a spa package?

Packages combine several services, so we reserve them by phone at (801) 501-9000 to line up your therapist, esthetician and nail technician. Single services can be booked online.

How much does a spa package cost in Draper?

Signature packages at Falling Waters start at $207 for The Ultimate Spa Day, $210 for The Birthday Glow Experience and $349 for The Bloom & Bliss Couples Experience. Treehouse Athletic Club members save 10%.

How long does a package take?

Plan about three hours for The Ultimate Spa Day or The Birthday Glow Experience, and about 90 minutes for the Bloom & Bliss couples massage including check-in.

Can I give a package as a gift?

Yes. Gift cards are available for every package amount and can be purchased by phone or in person.
