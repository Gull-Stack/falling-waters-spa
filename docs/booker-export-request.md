# Booker data export — the note Erika forwards

Falling Waters sends this to Booker (Mindbody) support from the spa's own
account email. Booker only releases data to the account holder.

---

**Subject:** Data export request — Falling Waters Day Spa & Salon (location: fallingwaters)

Hi Booker team,

We're moving Falling Waters Day Spa & Salon (Draper, UT; Booker location
"fallingwaters") to a new booking system. Please send a full export of our
account data as CSV files:

1. Customers: name, email, phone, birthday, notes, marketing opt-in.
2. Gift certificates / gift cards: code, original value, remaining balance, purchase date, recipient.
3. Future appointments: date, time, service, provider, customer, notes, deposit paid.
4. Series / packages with remaining sessions, by customer.
5. Services and providers, with durations and prices.

Please also confirm our contract renewal date and the notice needed to cancel.

Thank you,
[Name], Falling Waters Day Spa & Salon

---

**Why each file matters**
- Customers: guests keep their history, so nobody books as a stranger.
- Gift cards: the spa's top seller, and a liability. Every open balance must
  redeem on day one.
- Future appointments: loaded into Cinch before the "Book" buttons switch,
  so the front desk runs one book.
